## Photo placement and admin controls

- Home: real featured artwork, Quiet Memory in the hero, and an Art & Community photo strip.
- About: Kira_Art_Artist_02.jpg in The Artist Behind the Canvas; the previous artist photograph remains on the homepage.
- Collections: an Art & Community photo strip.
- Artwork details: matching community photographs where the depicted painting is identifiable.
- Exhibitions & Events: all 27 unique gathering photographs in an expandable gallery and full-size viewer with keyboard navigation. No unverified location, date, or visitor names are added.
- Community: selected gathering photo previews, linked to Exhibitions & Events.
- Admin: acrylic palette, feathered logos, real artwork previews, and a Pages & images editor with upload/replacement, fit, zoom, position, gallery add/remove, and featured artwork controls.
